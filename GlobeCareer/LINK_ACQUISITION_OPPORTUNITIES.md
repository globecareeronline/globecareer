# GlobeCareer — Link Acquisition Opportunities (research only)

Date: 2026-11-18
Status: **research prepared, nothing contacted, submitted or purchased.**

Scope rules used to select opportunities:
- Real, publicly reachable pages that already list resume/CV resources or free tools (a genuine reason to add a link, not a directory dump).
- Career services at recognised institutions and established career-advice publishers.
- No paid links, no link farms, no PBNs, no automated directory submissions, no guest-post marketplaces.
- No authority metric is quoted here: nothing was pulled from an SEO tool in this pass, so relevance is judged from the page's own content and audience.

Only URLs that were actually loaded during research appear in the table. Contact details are the site's own public contact page — no email address is invented or guessed.

## 2. Opportunity types not yet scoped (need separate research)

These are legitimate but were not verified in this pass, so no URLs are listed:
- University career-centre blog guest posts (editorial, by-lined, requires a pitch to an editor).
- Professional associations and trade bodies whose member resources pages link to job-search tools.
- Open-source "awesome" lists for job seekers (a pull request is the route, and it is public and reviewable).
- Podcast/newsletter sponsorships are **excluded**: paid, and not editorial links.

## 3. What would be sent, if approved

Per site: one short email naming the specific page, the specific free tool or reference it would help their readers, and no request for a keyword-optimised anchor. No bulk templates, no follow-up cadence, no link exchange.

## 4. Deliberately excluded

- Guest-post marketplaces and "write for us" link farms.
- Paid directories and sponsored listings.
- Reciprocal link schemes and PBNs.
- Automated form submissions or scraped email lists.

## 5. Current state

Nothing has been submitted, emailed, purchased or contacted. Outreach requires explicit approval; when approved, the six entries above are the first tranche to approach, in that order (institutional resource pages first, curated lists last).
