INSTRUCTIONS FOR GOOGLE AI STUDIO
------------------------------------
GLOBECareer SEO IMPLEMENTATION BRIEF
Use the attached keyword master workbook as research input, not as instructions to blindly insert every keyword.
1. Audit the existing GlobeCareer codebase, routes, page content, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt, metadata, structured data, internal links, and current functionality before changing anything.
2. Use the workbook's Keyword, Intent, Volume (US), KD (%), Cluster, Suggested Target Page, Priority, and Implementation Note columns.
3. First build a keyword-to-URL map. Choose one primary keyword/topic for each important page and assign related variants naturally. Avoid keyword stuffing, duplicate pages, doorway pages, and keyword cannibalization.
4. Homepage: select one accurate primary brand/product intent based on the actual product (resume/CV builder if that is the core feature). Do not try to target all job-board, cover-letter, and country-specific keywords on the homepage.
5. Preserve existing features, design, data, authentication, export functionality, and routes. Do not claim features (job listings, AI scoring, cover letter generation, trackers, etc.) that are not actually implemented and working.
6. Country-specific keywords require genuinely localized, accurate guidance and templates. Do not mass-generate thin country pages.
7. Improve on-page SEO where useful: unique titles and meta descriptions, one clear H1, helpful headings, descriptive copy, image alt text, crawlable internal links, breadcrumbs where appropriate, canonical tags, and relevant valid structured data.
8. Audit technical SEO: indexability, status codes, canonical consistency, sitemap/robots alignment, mobile usability, page performance, broken links, duplicate content, redirects, and rendered metadata.
9. Add internal links between relevant existing pages with natural descriptive anchor text. Do not create sitewide exact-match anchor spam.
10. Off-page SEO: do not fabricate backlinks or claim links have been acquired. Prepare a legitimate link earning plan around useful free tools, original career resources, outreach, partnerships, and digital PR. Do not use spam directories, paid link schemes, or automated link blasts.
11. Do not promise rankings or guaranteed traffic. Search volume and KD are third-party estimates, not guaranteed results.
12. Before edits, create/verify a recoverable backup. Implement in small batches, run available build/tests, verify important pages in light/dark mode and mobile/desktop, and report changed files, tests, remaining gaps, and any work requiring Search Console or external account access. Do not deploy without approval.
